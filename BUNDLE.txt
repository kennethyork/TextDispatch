LSPDFR IN TEXT — the bundle
===========================

Two plugins that are meant to be installed together:

    TextDispatch %DISPATCH_VERSION%    the text interface: dispatch, the people in the callouts,
                                       traffic stops, records, all through a chat box
    TextCallouts %CALLOUTS_VERSION%    eight callouts, written from scratch, needing nothing but
                                       LSPDFR and RAGE Plugin Hook

Both DLLs are in this zip. Neither RAGE Plugin Hook nor LSPDFR is - they are separate works under
their own licences, and you need your own working install of both.


INSTALL
-------

1. Copy BOTH DLLs into:

       <your Grand Theft Auto V folder>\Plugins\LSPDFR\

   Create the folder if it is not there. Plugins\LSPDFR - not Plugins, and not the game's lspdfr\
   data folder. Only that folder is loaded by LSPDFR into its own AppDomain, which is what lets a
   callout pack see the callout API at all.

2. Start the game with RagePluginHook.exe, not the normal launcher.

3. Load story mode, then GO ON DUTY: press E at a police station.

   This is the step people miss. LSPDFR loads everything in Plugins\LSPDFR at that moment and not
   before it - no chat box, no callouts, nothing, however long you wait in story mode.

4. The box appears saying "TextDispatch loaded. Press T to chat." Press T.

   TextCallouts registers its eight callouts at the same moment and tells you so on screen.


WHAT THEY DO TOGETHER
---------------------

Installed side by side, they are one experience:

  * the eight callouts arrive as radio traffic in the chat box, and you accept or decline by typing
  * every line a callout says - the ambulance request, the pursuit call-in, the suspect description -
    is mirrored into the chat box instead of a notification box
  * /calls lists all eight alongside LSPDFR's own callouts and any other pack's, and /callout <name>
    starts one on demand, which is the quickest way to see one
  * people in the callouts answer in text when you talk to them
  * and your own callouts - written as XML files in Plugins\LSPDFR\TextCallouts\Custom\ - are
    offered exactly like the built-in eight, no compiling involved

None of that is a hard dependency. Each plugin works alone: TextDispatch runs with no callout pack,
and TextCallouts runs with no text interface - the callout lines simply arrive as normal
notifications instead. The link is made at runtime by reflection, so installing one without the other
breaks nothing.


CHECKING IT
-----------

Rage Plugin Hook's log (RagePluginHook.log, and dated copies in Logs\) shows what loaded. Look for
"Creating plugin: TextDispatch.Main" and "Creating plugin: TextCallouts.Main" - they only appear
after you go on duty.

    Plugins\LSPDFR\textdispatch.log    what loaded, and what each plugin in the folder is missing
    Plugins\LSPDFR\textcallouts.log    which callouts registered, what each one said, which native ran

In the chat box:  /plugins  /calls  /help
In the F4 console: tdstatus, tdplugins, tcstatus, tccallouts


REQUIREMENTS
------------

Grand Theft Auto V (Legacy or Enhanced), RAGE Plugin Hook, LSPDFR 0.4.9 or newer, .NET Framework 4.8.

Optional, for TextDispatch's conversation: a local model on your own machine - Ollama on
localhost:11434, or LM Studio. TextDispatch works without one, on a built-in script.

Nothing else is required. In particular TextCallouts needs no RAGENativeUI, no StopThePed, no
Ultimate Backup, no CompuLite, no Callout Interface, no External PoliceComputer, no Common Data
Framework - the complete list of assemblies it references is LSPD First Response, RagePluginHook and
the .NET framework.
